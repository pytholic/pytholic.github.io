Facial Part Segmentation

2021-2022

Part segmentation of 3D meshes. Segmenting nose and ear region mainly in facial point clouds. Generally, the segmentation task is more complicated than classification and detection tasks. This situation aggravates further in medical and point cloud domains.

In this project, I explored the application of both traditional and modern machine learning algorithms on point cloud data to segment various parts in the facial point cloud of patients.
