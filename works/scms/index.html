Smart In-cabin Monitoring System

2022-2023

Developed a Smart In-cabin Monitoring System based on a single fish-eye camera and computer vision techniques. Some of the main responsibilities were data collection, model development, training and evaluation, model export, and quantization. This projects aim to improve safety of passengers in autonomous vehicles by providing a complete and robust real-time monitoring system.
